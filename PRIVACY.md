# Privacy Notes

This workflow can process names, email addresses, company information, budgets, timelines, and free-text business enquiries.

The operator is responsible for providing an appropriate privacy notice, establishing a lawful basis, minimising collected data, setting a retention period, handling deletion requests, and documenting all processors, including the n8n host, model provider, marketplace, and logging services.

Do not use this workflow for sensitive personal data without a separate privacy and security assessment.
